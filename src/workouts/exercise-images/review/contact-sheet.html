Monday · undefinedbarbell-bench-press

Barbell Bench Press

Primary: Pectoralis major

Secondary: Triceps brachii, Anterior deltoid

Equipment: Barbell, Flat bench, Rack with safety arms

Difficulty / reference: Intermediate · 3 × 8–12

Starting position: Lie on the flat bench with eyes under the racked bar, feet supported on the floor, shoulder blades set against the bench, and hands gripping the bar slightly wider than shoulder width.

Technique: Unrack with assistance or after confirming the safeties are set; position the bar over the mid-chest.
Lower under control until the bar lightly touches the mid-chest while wrists remain over elbows.
Press upward without bouncing the bar and return it to the rack only after the elbows are extended.

Common mistakes: Bouncing the bar off the chest.
Losing foot support or letting the wrists fold backward.

Safety: Use a competent spotter or correctly adjusted safety arms; stop if shoulder or chest pain occurs.

Monday · undefinedincline-dumbbell-press

Incline Dumbbell Press

Primary: Pectoralis major, clavicular portion

Secondary: Anterior deltoid, Triceps brachii

Equipment: Dumbbells, Adjustable incline bench

Difficulty / reference: Beginner · 3 × 10–12

Starting position: Set the bench to a comfortable shallow incline, sit with dumbbells resting on the thighs, then lie back with the weights beside the upper chest and feet supported.

Technique: Press both dumbbells upward over the upper chest without forcefully locking the elbows.
Lower with control until the upper arms approach the bench plane and the shoulders remain comfortable.

Common mistakes: Using a very steep bench angle that turns the movement into a shoulder press.
Allowing the dumbbells to drift far behind the shoulders.

Safety: Use a load that can be controlled through the full range; place the dumbbells down safely before sitting up.

Flat Dumbbell Press

Primary: Pectoralis major

Secondary: Triceps brachii, Anterior deltoid

Equipment: Pair of dumbbells, Flat bench

Difficulty / reference: Beginner · 3 × 10–12

Starting position: Lie on a flat bench with feet supported; hold dumbbells over the chest with wrists stacked above elbows.

Monday · undefinedcable-chest-fly

Cable Chest Fly

Primary: Pectoralis major, clavicular portion

Secondary: Anterior deltoid

Equipment: Dual adjustable cable station, Two handles

Difficulty / reference: Beginner · 3 × 12–15

Starting position: Stand centered between low pulleys with a split stance, handles beside the hips, elbows softly bent, and trunk upright.

Technique: Sweep the handles forward and upward in a broad arc toward upper-chest height while keeping the elbow bend nearly constant.
Return the handles outward and downward slowly until the chest is stretched without the shoulders being pulled forward.

Common mistakes: Turning the movement into a press by repeatedly bending and straightening the elbows.
Arching the lower back to move a heavier load.

Safety: Use light, controllable resistance and a comfortable shoulder range; do not force a deep stretch.

Rope Pushdown

Primary: Triceps brachii

Secondary: Anconeus

Tuesday · undefineddeadlift

Deadlift

Primary: Gluteus maximus, Hamstrings

Secondary: Quadriceps, Spinal erectors, Trapezius

Equipment: Barbell, Weight plates, Clear floor space

Difficulty / reference: Advanced · 3 × 5–8

Starting position: Stand with the bar over mid-foot, hinge and bend the knees to reach the bar, and brace with a neutral trunk before lifting.

Technique: Push through the floor and extend hips and knees together while keeping the bar close to the legs.
Stand tall without leaning back, then hinge and bend the knees to return the bar under control.

Common mistakes: Rounding the trunk, jerking the bar from the floor, or letting it drift forward.
Overextending the back at lockout.

Safety: Requires technique instruction and a clear, pain-free hinge; do not assign a starting load. Stop for back pain, numbness, or loss of control.
